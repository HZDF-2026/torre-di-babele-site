Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies (and similar technologies such as local storage) are small files that a website or its providers can store in your browser. This page explains which of them are involved when you use this website. It is part of the service run by Kunming Unintellix Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd.; see also our Privacy Policy.

2. What we set

None. We set no cookies and no local storage of our own: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no preference storage. The website works fully with cookies blocked.

3. Third parties involved

  • Cloudflare (hosting). Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary security cookies (for example __cf_bm) to distinguish human visitors from automated traffic. These are needed to serve the site and protect it from abuse; because they are strictly necessary, no consent is required. Blocking them may prevent pages from loading.
  • Google Fonts. Page styling loads typefaces from Google's font servers. The provider may log the request (for example your IP address and browser headers). No cookies are set for this by our website.
  • Payment providers. When checkout becomes available, the payment provider (for example a Merchant of Record) will set its own essential cookies during the purchase flow. We will update this page before that happens, and we will ask for your consent before enabling any cookie category that requires it.

4. Managing cookies

You can view, block, or delete cookies in your browser's settings, usually under "Privacy & Security" → "Cookies" (the exact wording varies by browser). Because we set none ourselves, clearing cookies affects only the strictly necessary items above.

5. Do Not Track

We do not run tracking, so we do not respond to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals — there is nothing for them to switch off.

6. Changes to this Policy

If the technologies described here change, we will update this page with a new "last updated" date.
